💡 Understanding GPS Data Issues

Why GPS data might be zeroed/missing:

  • ✅ Original photo: GPS data intact
  • ❌ Social media photo: GPS removed (Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram)
  • ❌ Edited photo: GPS stripped by editing apps
  • ❌ Privacy settings: Location disabled in camera app
  • ❌ File transfer: GPS lost during sharing

Solution: Always use original photos from your Camera folder, not from social/media apps.
